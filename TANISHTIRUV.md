# Friend Match — loyihani tanishtirish

## Qisqacha

Friend Match — o‘smirlarning xarakteri va qiziqishlarini solishtirish orqali ideal do‘stlikni tasavvur qilishga yordam beradigan o‘quv veb-ilova. Loyiha 10-sinf ingliz tili BSB-1 topshirig‘ining to‘rt bosqichini bitta interaktiv muhitda birlashtiradi.

Oddiy profil ro‘yxatidan farqli ravishda, foydalanuvchi tanlovini tushuntiradi: menga qanday do‘st kerak, nima uchun aynan shu sifat muhim, nimaga birga vaqt ajratishimiz mumkin va farqlarimizni qanday yengamiz?

## Kimlar uchun?

- “Design the Perfect Friend” loyihasini bajaradigan 10-sinf o‘quvchilari.
- B1 darajasida xarakter va do‘stlik mavzusida inglizcha gapirishni mashq qiluvchilar.
- Kichik guruhlarda faoliyat tashkil qiladigan ingliz tili o‘qituvchilari.

## Darsda nimalarni ko‘rsatish mumkin?

Avval Amina nomli xayoliy qahramonni ko‘rsating. Uning xarakteri, qiziqishlari, kuchli va zaif tomonlarini tushuntiring. Keyin ideal do‘stning beshta sifatini sabablar bilan tanishtiring. Masalan, sabrli do‘st Amina shoshilganda unga xotirjamroq qaror qilishga yordam berishi mumkin.

So‘ng Hana yoki boshqa namuna profilni tanlang. Beshta savolga javob berib, 50 ballik natijani oching. Qaysi mezonda kuchli moslik borligi, qaysi joyda kelishib olish kerakligini izohlang. Yakunda Class presentation orqali beshta slaydni ko‘rsating.

## Qisqa o‘zbekcha tanishtiruv nutqi

Assalomu alaykum! Bizning loyihamiz “Friend Match” deb nomlanadi. Uning maqsadi xarakter va umumiy qiziqishlar do‘stlikka qanday ta’sir qilishini o‘rganishdir.

Ilovada avval xayoliy o‘smir profilini yaratamiz. Uning yaxshi tomonlarini ham, rivojlantirishi kerak bo‘lgan tomonlarini ham yozamiz. Keyin unga mos do‘stda bo‘lishi kerak bo‘lgan beshta sifatni tanlaymiz va sabablarini tushuntiramiz.

Besh savolli test turli vaziyatlarda qanday yo‘l tutishimizni ko‘rsatadi. Natija xarakter, umumiy qiziqishlar, ishonch, muloqot va birga maroqli vaqt o‘tkazish bo‘yicha hisoblanadi. Har bir mezon 10 ball, jami 50 ball.

Loyiha ingliz tilidagi sifatlar, prefikslar va fe’lning -ing shakllarini qo‘llashga yordam beradi. Yakunda beshta slayd asosida guruh bo‘lib taqdimot o‘tkazamiz. Barcha profillar xayoliy; ilova haqiqiy odamlar bilan yozishma xizmati emas. Bizning xulosamiz: yaxshi do‘stlik umumiy qiziqishlar bilan boshlanishi mumkin, ammo ishonch, hurmat va tinglash bilan rivojlanadi.

## Short English presentation — B1

Hello everyone! Our project is called Friend Match. It is a simple classroom app about friendship, personality and shared interests.

First, we create a fictional teenager. Our example is Amina, who is sixteen. She is creative, curious, outgoing and loyal. However, she can also be impatient and disorganized. She enjoys drawing, loves meeting new people and does not mind helping a friend.

Next, we describe her ideal friend. Amina needs someone patient, honest, supportive, open-minded and reliable. A patient friend can help her slow down. An honest friend makes it easier to build trust. We do not want a dishonest or insensitive friend because these qualities can hurt a friendship.

Our app has five original questions. They explore personality, common interests, trust, communication and having fun together. Each category has a maximum of ten points, so the total is fifty.

After the test, we explain the result and discuss possible problems. A high score does not guarantee a perfect friendship. Both people still need to listen, respect differences and keep their promises.

Finally, we present our ideas in five slides. All characters are fictional, and there is no real messaging. Thank you for listening!
